About

How it works
  1. Pick your grade, subject and language on the Ask tab.
  2. Type your doubt or add a photo of the question, then get a step-by-step explanation.
  3. Use Explain simpler, Another example or Read aloud if you need more help.
  4. Take the quiz, then check your score on the Progress tab.
Good to know
  • Your progress is saved only in this browser.
  • AI explanations can sometimes be wrong, so check important answers with your teacher or textbook.
  • This is a student project prototype built for IBM SkillsBuild.
